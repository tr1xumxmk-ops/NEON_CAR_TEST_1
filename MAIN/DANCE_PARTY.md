# SPARK CAR — Dance Party and Cute Reactions

The floating companion now has more than one dance style. Use **DANCE PARTY** to start a character-specific routine, or let autonomous reactions choose from the expanded event pool. PICO gets a bouncy, clumsy, affectionate routine; BEEP gets a robotic boogie, a precise slide, a recalibration spin, a brief rhythm panic, and an embarrassed finish.

## Added moves

- Moonwalk
- Side-to-side slide
- Wiggle
- Hop-hop
- Spin / twirl
- Robot-bop
- Panic dance
- Victory dance
- Peekaboo
- Tiptoe sneak
- Tiny tumble
- Applause / clapping
- Cozy cuddle
- Heart dance
- Sparkle chase
- Imaginary confetti

## Sound and safety
These routines use CSS/SVG animation and Web Audio in the browser. Sound effects require the companion sound toggle to be enabled and the browser audio context to be unlocked by a click or tap. The dances do not issue motor commands and do not increase ESP32 request frequency. For motor testing, raise the wheels and keep the physical power disconnect accessible.
