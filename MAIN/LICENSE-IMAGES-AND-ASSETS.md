# Images, faces, sounds, and other assets

- The robot faces in this edition are inline SVG markup authored for the SPARK CAR dashboard; they are not user portraits or third-party photographs.
- Dashboard graphics are CSS/SVG drawn in the page; no external image CDN or remote font is required.
- Browser sound patterns are synthesized by JavaScript/Web Audio and are not bundled audio recordings.
- The dashboard screenshot and SVG banner in `docs/assets/readme/` are project documentation graphics.
- Do not add team photos, logos, fonts, sounds, or downloaded assets unless the project has permission to redistribute them. Record attribution and license details when third-party assets are added.
